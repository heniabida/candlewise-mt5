# Change log

## 1.00: first release (November 2026)

### [Candlewise Lite 1.00](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/product/207061)

- 22 classic candlestick patterns: engulfing, harami, harami cross, piercing line, dark cloud cover, morning and evening star, morning and evening doji star, three white soldiers, three black crows, three inside up/down, three outside up/down, hammer, hanging man, inverted hammer, shooting star, doji, dragonfly doji, gravestone doji, tweezer top and bottom.
- Pattern zones and labels on the chart; labels are placed in free space so they don't cover candles or each other.
- Strength score (1–5) from the prior move and swing high/low context.
- Confirmation status shown on each zone: waiting, confirmed, failed or unconfirmed.
- Light and dark themes. The chart is restyled to match, and your own chart colours come back when you remove the indicator.

### [Candlewise Pro 1.00](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/product/207067)

Everything in Lite, plus:

- 63 candlestick patterns.
- Pattern data your own Expert Advisor can read through `iCustom`: direction, strength and pattern id (no EA included).
- On-chart toolbar: bullish, bearish and indecision filters, minimum strength, label mode, zones, theme, panel.
- Pattern card with context, confirmation, history on this symbol and the invalidation price.
- Optional preview of the pattern forming on the open candle, marked "forming".
- Scanner for up to 12 symbols and 9 timeframes; click a cell to open that chart.
- Signal feed with live status.
- Pattern Lab: how often each pattern appeared on this symbol and how price moved afterwards, next to a baseline for any candle.
- Library: switch any pattern on or off, saved for every chart.
- Alerts when a candle closes: popup, sound, push notification and email.
